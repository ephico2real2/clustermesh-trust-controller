CMTC · eBPF measurement plane · ADR-005 · 2026-10-25

The measurement plane measures; Cilium and ztunnel decide

Cilium owns packet enforcement and the quarantine gate. Ztunnel owns workload TLS and authorization. The CMTC eBPF programs only count. The observer is the first, single-workload increment. The fence is a lab-only deny tool. The node-wide plane is proposed.
