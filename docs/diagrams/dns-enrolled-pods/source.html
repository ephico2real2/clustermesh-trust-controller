CMTC · DNS for enrolled Pods · 2026-10-26

DNS never waits for mTLS

An enrolled Pod resolves names in plaintext, straight to CoreDNS, without ztunnel and without a certificate. The Cilium HBONE-only gate lets that plaintext through only on port 53 and only to an approved resolver identity. The request that follows the answer is the one that travels over HBONE mTLS.
