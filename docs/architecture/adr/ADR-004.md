**Status:** Proposed; decision direction from static source review on 2026-10-23, current state added 2026-10-25. Architecture/security approval pending.

## Decision direction

Apply platform protection → explicit exclusion → explicit selection mode → owned label reconciliation → observed enforcement. Support AllEligible and MatchSelectors; OR across selectors, AND within each selector. Use existing LOB/mnemonic metadata. Exclusions never grant access to protected destinations.

## Source evidence and gaps

V02 verifies the native label; CMTC precedence, admission and field ownership are unimplemented. Cilium's shown table keys by namespace name; CMTC must bind namespace UID and generation separately.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

Require a declared selection mode. Define empty/invalid selectors, multiple-policy conflicts, reserved-label/exemption permissions and GitOps ownership before generating CRDs. Do not remove another actor's labels.

## Current state (2026-10-25)

- CMTC evaluates the precedence in Observe mode only: platform, exclusion and selection decisions are counted in policy status (`internal/policy/evaluate.go`, `internal/controller/policy.go`).
- The controller never changes namespace labels; it sets `EnforcementVerified=Unknown` with reason `CiliumIntegrationUnavailable` (`internal/controller/policy.go:43`).
- The lab's enrolled namespace `cmtc-live-mtls` was labelled by the operational fixture runner with `kubectl patch`, not by CMTC. Owned label reconciliation, admission and field ownership remain unimplemented.

## Acceptance gates

Unit/envtest cases cover precedence, selectors, UID recreation, relabeling, drift and ownership. Unsupported enforcement blocks live label changes; label success never becomes Protected. Missing platform inventory or conflicts produce explicit reasons.

Evidence IDs refer to the [Cilium v1.20.4 audit](../../research/SOURCE_AUDIT.md). The current-state section reports deployed and tested facts; it does not claim `Protected` or qualify any gate below. Mark Accepted only after explicit architecture/security approval.
