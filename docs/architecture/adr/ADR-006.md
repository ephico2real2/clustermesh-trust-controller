# ADR-006: Pod readiness and fail closed

**Status:** Proposed; updated from static source review on 2026-10-23. Architecture/security approval pending.

## Decision direction

Distinguish desired enrollment, label application, local enrollment, identity readiness and fresh enforcement evidence. Protected requires verified Cilium enforcement and expected peer identity/policy for the supported profile. Missing/stale evidence is Unknown, PendingEnforcement or Blocked with a reason.

## Source evidence and gaps

V03 shows asynchronous enrollment and a ZDS success/cache path; V07 shows empty authorization resources. Neither establishes first-packet safety or remote authorization. The endpoint-created callback logs failures; that is not a fail-closed guarantee.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

Admission/readiness cannot prevent arbitrary packets from running pods. Require a Cilium-owned gate through startup, relabel, trust-generation changes, restart and unenrollment; do not add broad pre-enrollment exceptions.

## Acceptance gates

Capture race stress, failed SVID/ZDS setup, UID recreation, proxy crashes and Cilium restore. Prove unauthorized traffic cannot escape before enforcement or during teardown. Expire status and bind it to UID/generation/version. Namespace status or a port-15008 connection cannot satisfy this gate.

Evidence IDs refer to the [Cilium v1.20.4 audit](../../research/SOURCE_AUDIT.md). No runtime capability is claimed. Mark Accepted only after explicit architecture/security approval.
