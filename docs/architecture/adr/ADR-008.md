# ADR-008: Metrics and SLOs

**Status:** Proposed; updated from static source review on 2026-10-23. Architecture/security approval pending.

## Decision direction

Preserve nine Grafana dashboards, bounded aggregate labels and logs/events/Hubble detail. Separate desired, labeled, locally enrolled and verified protection. Keep SLOs provisional until denominators, scope and missing-data behavior are approved.

## Source evidence and gaps

V12 identifies native enrollment/local ZDS collectors. The [observability audit](../../../observability/docs/SOURCE_AUDIT.md) records absent CMTC exporters, global aggregations, ambiguous inventory/rate semantics, absent latency panels and no-data gaps. No live contract is validated.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

Reuse native collectors only for their actual semantics after scrape verification. Compare explicit observer health/expected-cluster inventory with zero-error ratios. Label counts, ZDS connections and handshakes cannot substitute for authorization or dual encryption.

## Acceptance gates

Approve types, labels, source, freshness, owner and cardinality. Implement exporters and verify samples. Promtool checks/fixtures and all nine Grafana imports cover zero traffic, absent series/exporters/clusters, denial and failure. No aggregate pod UID/SPIFFE/LOB labels.

Evidence IDs refer to the [Cilium v1.20.4 audit](../../research/SOURCE_AUDIT.md). No runtime capability is claimed. Mark Accepted only after explicit architecture/security approval.
