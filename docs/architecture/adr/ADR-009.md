# ADR-009: Rollout and recovery

**Status:** Proposed; updated from static source review on 2026-10-23. Architecture/security approval pending.

## Decision direction

Use preflight, limited canary, health gates and verified recovery. Routine rotation requires old+new public-bundle consumption, safe issuance switch and drain before retirement. Define a separate emergency compromise workflow.

## Source evidence and gaps

V01/G03/G04/G06 block automatic production rollout. Removing a label or creating a bundle ConfigMap proves neither recovery nor reload. Runtime rollback, partitions and revocation remain untested.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

Prefer explicit Blocked status over progress through missing evidence. A disconnected required cluster blocks routine retirement; membership removal needs authorization/audit. Emergency revocation may reduce availability and cannot silently use routine rollback.

## Acceptance gates

Exercise partitions, stale/forged ACKs, interrupted rotation, old connections, restart and platform degradation. Verify safe generation, consumer digests and platform recovery after rollback. Security review approves emergency isolation/revocation and a recovery owner before live rollout.

Evidence IDs refer to the [Cilium v1.20.4 audit](../../research/SOURCE_AUDIT.md). No runtime capability is claimed. Mark Accepted only after explicit architecture/security approval.
